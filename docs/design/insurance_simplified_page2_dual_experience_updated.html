About this tool

Understand the policy before you rely on it.

Insurance Simplified organizes information from documents you supply. It helps you inspect costs, limits, eligibility conditions and exclusions, and makes it easier to find the original wording.

Read one document

Choose any uploaded PDF and work through its details. Switching documents keeps the same upload session.

Compare documents

View up to three selected plans side by side, reorder the columns, and inspect the evidence behind supported differences.

Know what's uncertain

If the source does not establish a selected option or a plan relationship, the app should say so instead of guessing.

A necessary distinction

A brochure is not a confirmation of enrollment.

Available options are not necessarily purchased options. A quoted passage does not automatically prove which plan it applies to. Confirm the important details with the insurer before buying or relying on coverage.
